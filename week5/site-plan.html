Site Plan

Site Name: www.redemberwebdesign.com

This domain name represents the title of the business (RedEmber Web Design).
It uses the '.com' top-level domain signifying that the site is used for commercial purposes.

Site Purpose:

This site will allow prospective customers to see information about RedEmber Web Design and the various services it provides. It acts as a persuasive landing site for the business and contains a form for the user to contact the business owner for service.

Scenarios

  • Is RedEmber Web Design a good option for building or upgrading my website?
  • Why should I choose a freelance Web Designer over a service like Wix?
  • Is RedEmber Web Design a business that I can trust?
  • How can I reach out for services or for a quote?

Color Scheme

  • Background: #ededed
  • Primary: #ffffff
  • Secondary: #565264
  • Complimentary Green: #668f76
  • Font Color: #2b303c;

Typography: Plus Jakarta Sans

This will be the only font used on the webpage.
Headings will be bold.
